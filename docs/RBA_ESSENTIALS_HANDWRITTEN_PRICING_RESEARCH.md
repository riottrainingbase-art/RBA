- Previous user requested that handwritten customization be **excluded from current normal-product presale**. The option stays disabled until a qualified craftsperson and washing/quality process are verified.

## Pricing proposal
- Standard short handwritten message, one garment, one placement, one pigment color, simple lettering: **+4,400 yen incl. tax, suggested**.
- Example totals *only if base apparel prices are approved unchanged*: white tee 4,900 + 4,400 = 9,300 yen; black setup 19,800 + 4,400 = 24,200 yen (one selected garment in the set, not both). Shipping separate, unknown.
- Longer lettering, two garment placements, complex illustrations, and an independently signed artist edition: quote separately, not included at +4,400.
- Reserve a right to reject disallowed personalization content and refund correctly; communicate spelling/proof process and placement restrictions.

## Illustrative marginal-cost check; NOT supplier quotation
Assume: personal handwriting/cure/inspection labor 45 minutes at 2,500 yen/hour = 1,875 yen; pigment/consumables 300 yen; preparation/packaging extra 200 yen; expected errors/reworks allowance 300 yen; incremental processing charges 150 yen. Total **2,825 yen**; estimated contribution before tax adjustment and shared overhead **1,575 yen** for +4,400 yen.
The estimate varies significantly with labor/cure duration, material testing, refunds, ink safety, and taxes. The contribution is NOT net profit, and it is not a confirmed financial forecast.
