# RBA BRAND IDENTITY / 2026–2036

2026-11-25 JST — representative approved brand direction in ChatGPT. This is the visual identity standard for Riot Basketball Academy and its related programmes, not a legal trademark registration.

## Non-negotiable
- Keep the original monochrome RBA emblem (public/rba-logo-original.jpg) unchanged. Do not recolor, redraw, stretch, or generate it.
- RBA UNITED, RBA JOURNAL, D-HUB, MY HOME COURT, DEVELOPMENT CAMP, GREAM and international programmes use the shared palette. Distinct partner marks and legally required/semantic warning, error, success and status colors remain intact.
- Do not use orange, brass, or lime as competing identity accents in new RBA-owned materials. Do not replace meaningful status colors indiscriminately.
- Use white text on RBA BLUE (6.30:1). RBA BLUE on COURT WHITE: 5.77:1. RIOT BLACK on COURT WHITE: 17.21:1. STEEL GRAY on COURT WHITE: 4.61:1. Blue on black is 2.98:1, so never use that pairing for essential text or standalone control boundaries; use white text and a contrasting border/focus indication. Gray on dark is not normal-size body text.
- Use authorized real activity photography, not fabricated documentary imagery. Retain partner colors and marks, consent and youth safeguarding.
- Apply to websites (all locales), MY HOME COURT, social templates, PDF/slide/print documents, proposals, invitations, uniforms, merchandise, signage, video and event artwork. For textile/print colors, approve physical samples; screen HEX is not a guaranteed Pantone/CMYK match. Preserve payment and safety functions.
- RBA is a youth development and international exchange initiative with non-commercial purpose; do not imply a registered legal status without verification. Purchase/participation/selection are independent.
- UNITED core copy: 今のチームのまま、世界へ。 / BEYOND YOUR TEAM. No migration or permanent membership required; project-specific eligibility applies. チームではなく、機会をつくる。

## Implementation and review
Use shared CSS variables --rba-blue, --rba-black, --rba-white, --rba-gray. Maintain responsive legibility and WCAG 2.2 AA, check mobile and all languages; do not change application, fees, or payment endpoints as part of rebranding. Before publication, build and visually verify. Existing documents and products are migrated in future revisions rather than silently modified. Approval of colors does not authorize a logo replacement.

## Participation and publication
- Label free interest as nonbinding, not an application, reservation, or payment; official entry requires verified eligibility, guardian consent, current fees, safety and payment terms. Advance payment is the default where approved; payment alone does not guarantee acceptance.
- Display verified dates, locations, scope, currency, refunds and deadlines from the authoritative programme record. Pending arrangements remain pending; never imply confirmed partner participation.
- Keep public recruitment separate from sensitive youth data. Use approved imagery, guardian consent, role-restricted records and qualified travel partners where required.
- For any release: verify all four locales, mobile layout, valid CTA destination, accessibility, current event/price, consent and cancellation disclosure, registration-to-payment reconciliation in a safe test environment, and production status. No live test charges.
