## One-page sponsor proposal template (company-specific)
1. **Why this company:** one cited company-owned CSR/sports activity, and how our age group differs.
2. **Problem:** young players need meaningful playing opportunities and safe cross-border learning, not travel for its own sake.
3. **Pilot:** one Japan and one Taiwan U12/U15 event planned in 2027; no dates or bookings promised.
4. **Specific support ask:** [exact cash or in-kind item only after cost quote and applicant eligibility].
5. **Benefit to participants:** number of subsidy places, hours of coach education or travel burden reduced — *targets* not guaranteed outcomes.
6. **Sponsor deliverables:** approved acknowledgment, permitted logo, post-event report; no minor data access.
7. **Safeguards:** parental permission, local event operators, insurance, emergency response, travel compliance, refunds.
8. **Evidence:** RBA 2026 Taiwan journal and official sponsor's own CSR page.
9. **CTA:** permission to share a short project brief or identify correct sponsorship department; no meeting-first pressure.
