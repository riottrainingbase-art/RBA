# RBA UNITED ASIA 2027 / DEVELOPMENT VISION

Different Teams. Different Countries. One Future.

RBA UNITED is an individual-entry, non-profit-purpose youth development project. No player must change their home team. Taiwan, Malaysia and the Philippines are focus markets for exploration, not confirmed 2027 destinations or partner contracts.

## Market pathways

| Focus market | Rationale | Proposed entry path |
|---|---|---|
| Taiwan | Existing Japan–Taiwan development exchanges | Reciprocal individual training and game experience |
| Malaysia | Academy and club contact opportunities | International game experience and reciprocal exchange |
| Philippines | Youth basketball club and organiser relationships | Local and inbound Japan development opportunities |

Public reference: [SPARTANS Japan–Taiwan coach profile](https://spartans.tw/en/ma/); [Malaysia Basketball Association — FIBA](https://about.fiba.basketball/en/regions/asia/national-federations/881-malaysia). These references do not imply endorsement of UNITED ASIA 2027.

## Pathways and boundaries

- UNITED: individually registered players; project-specific temporary teams.
- CONNECT: team/academy exchange; not individual registration.
- BACKBONE: 3x3 development.
- RIZEHOPE: tournaments.
- D-HUB: continuing development and coach education.
- MY HOME COURT: integrated platform and growth records.

## Delivery model

1. Free interest and guardian enquiry.
2. Verify eligibility, host, dates, staff, safety, insurance, cost, cancellation and legal travel arrangement.
3. Official application and advance payment where approved; no guaranteed place until verified.
4. Orientation, training/game experience and return reflection.
5. Continue learning at the home club; record participation in MY HOME COURT where available.

No confirmed destination, partner logo, date, capacity, fee or payment link may be added without supporting evidence and approval. Separate youth sporting participation from licensed travel arrangements. Do not publish personal information about minors.
