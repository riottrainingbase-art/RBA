# RTB Growth Engine — 90 Days

## North Star
Recognition is not the end goal. The operating funnel is:
REACH → RTB START → VISIT → PAID → RECURRING → REFERRAL.

## 90-day targets
- 60 RTB START leads
- 25 intro / assessment visits
- 10 new personal clients
- 20 membership preregistrations
- 12 qualified Team / Corporate leads
- 6 qualified Product Test leads
- 10 total Google reviews target (baseline 4)

## Channel allocation
- 50% local awareness / acquisition
- 25% conversion / personal & assessment
- 15% B2B
- 7% SELECT
- 3% OEM

## Google Business Profile
- 2 useful posts/week
- Keep Personal Trainer as primary category
- Do not change address casually because re-verification risk exists
- Ask satisfied customers for honest reviews; never incentivize positive reviews
- Use UTM-tagged appointment links
- Measure impressions, website clicks, directions, calls, reviews

## Instagram RTB
Content mix:
- 40% people / real training / facility
- 25% education
- 20% Sendai/local context
- 10% offer
- 5% SELECT
Primary CTA: RTB START / INTRO 15.
Do not turn the account into a product catalogue.

## Referral
Existing customers can refer only with the referred person's consent.
No reward is required during validation. Measure referral→visit→paid conversion first.

## B2B
Each week:
- 10 local Team targets
- 10 local Corporate targets
- 5 relevant Brand targets
Do not mass-send generic mail. Use role-specific offers and one clear CTA.

## Weekly review
Every Monday review:
1. GBP impressions/actions
2. IG reach/profile visits
3. START leads
4. visits
5. paid clients
6. B2B qualified leads
7. SELECT orders
8. revenue
Cut channels/content that create reach without leads after sufficient sample size.
