## Before opening paid preorders
1. Get written 3 / 5 / 10 bottle total supplier quotes including freight; resale / online / pre-order permissions; payment terms; stock and dispatch SLA; damages/returns; direct-shipping option if any.
2. Confirm RTB pickup arrangements, customer-facing terms, no surprise shipping, advertising claims, cancellation/refunds, and statutory seller disclosure before launch.
3. Check real expressions of intent from survey and minimum batch. Prefer at least 3 *paid* confirmed units in one short order window only after public terms are finalized; no speculative inventory.
4. Choose final price using confirmed all-in cost and competitor market checks. Avoid false urgency or implying vendor endorsement.
5. If feasible, use an appropriate merchant's payment flow; **do not** use an unrelated Stripe account or create an active payment link until the seller/account are verified.
6. Record actual landed cost, payment fees, sell-through, refunds, and net contribution for first batch; stop if negative or if low demand makes the effort uneconomic.
