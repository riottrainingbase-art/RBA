## 3. Unit economics before approval

Definitions:
- P = additional customer charge incl. consumption tax.
- tax rate = 10% **assumption**, verify domestic tax status/registration with seller.
- V = P/(1+tax rate), additional revenue net of included tax *if applicable*.
- Artist labor = actual minutes/60 × approved rate/h; payment to an artist is required even when artwork supports youth development.
- Unit cost = labor + pigments/consumables + transfer/protective supplies + special quality inspection + packaging difference + expected failure/rework allowance + incremental payment fee + any licensing/royalty fee.
- Estimated contribution = V − unit cost. This is a scenario, NOT legal taxable profit or guaranteed margin.
- The price must cover seller administration, packing, refunds and overhead separately; decline orders that need out-of-scope art.

Illustrative laboratory cases, NOT quotations:
- SIGNATURE: ¥4,400 tax-included; 45 min × ¥2,500/h = ¥1,875 labor; materials ¥300; inspection/packaging ¥250; expected rework ¥350; processing fee 3.6% of ¥4,400 = ¥158.40; approximate added contribution = ¥4,000 − (¥1,875+¥300+¥250+¥350+¥158) = **¥1,067** excluding business overhead.
- ARTIST ONE-OF-ONE: ¥8,800 tax-included; 100 min × ¥2,800/h = ¥4,667 labor; materials ¥500; inspection/packaging ¥350; expected rework ¥700; processing fee 3.6% of ¥8,800 = ¥317; approximate added contribution = ¥8,000 − (¥4,667+¥500+¥350+¥700+¥317) = **¥1,466** excluding royalties/overhead.

Actual Stripe pricing, consumption tax treatment, transfer/labor rates and returns must be verified. The formulas avoid double-counting garment costs; profit on base apparel is independently tracked.

## 4. Detailed physical specification and trials

1. Garment sampling: 1 × white cotton tee, 1 × black heavyweight fleece hoodie, plus a duplicate for destructive wash testing. Record fabric composition, GSM, weave, stretch, hand-feel, hidden seams, collar and size.
2. Confirm textile ink compatibility for *each* fabric (not ordinary stationery inks), secure supplier's safety data and manufacturer application / heat-curing instructions. Follow their specific curing parameters; do not invent a universal heat temperature.
3. Test candidate placement clear of official neck/hem brand markers, seams, hood and pockets. For sleeve/back variants, movement/abrasion stress is different. Photograph 1:1 garment, 45° oblique, close crop, and on-body with consent.
4. Record pre-wash and after 1/5/10/20 wash cycles under a documented protocol; evaluate fading, cracking, rubbing, bleed, skin irritation reports, shrinkage and warp. Define pass thresholds before test (e.g., no visible bleeding, no structural failure, legible text after 10 cycles). Do not claim washfastness until verified.
5. Test standardized writing text, ink amount, drying/heat curing time, one error/rework case, final inspection, photo proof and bagging. Clothing quality and care labels may require review post-processing, not just unchanged original tags.
6. Trial capacity with real times and throughput for 1, 5, 10 and 20 paid units; estimate extra lead time. When capacity is exceeded, close custom option, not ordinary-tee checkout.
7. Add complete artwork information to purchaser acceptance: handwritten variation, modest size/placement drift, writing and final spelling approval, changes/cancellation deadline, washing/care, defect/rework remedy, explicit shipping date.

## 7. Safety / consumer disclosure

- Japanese Consumer Affairs Agency requires conspicuous price, shipping, total fees, seller identity, method/time of payment, definite delivery period and cancellation/return terms before accepting distance sales. A bare 'about one month' may not be a sufficient delivery cutoff if actual calendar-based deadline is required; use a specific date/period confirmed by production scheduling. https://www.no-trouble.caa.go.jp/what/mailorder/index.html
- Prepaid orders with delayed delivery require acceptance notification; display and confirm signed terms. https://www.no-trouble.caa.go.jp/what/mailorder/
- Original textile labels may need re-evaluation if a paint process changes care requirements. See CAA household textile listing and operations: https://www.caa.go.jp/policies/policy/representation/household_goods/guide/fiber/
- Avoid “returns forbidden in every case”; distinguish defects, item mismatch, change-of-mind, and individualized painted item terms.
- Profit use should say accurately: **販売利益（製造費・作家報酬・運営経費等を差し引いた残額）をRBAの育成活動に充当する方針**. This is an ordinary merchandise purchase, not necessarily a deductible donation.
