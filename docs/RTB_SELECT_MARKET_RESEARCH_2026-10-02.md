# RTB SELECT — Market / Channel Research
Updated: 2026-10-02
Status: PREVIEW / INTERNAL
Owner: Riot Training Base

## Executive decision

RTB SELECT should NOT launch as a conventional sports shop and should NOT launch as an inventory-heavy apparel brand.

The strongest low-cash model is:

RTB = physical entrance / consultation / sample experience
RBA = basketball category + event/community distribution
Square Online Free = zero-monthly-cost checkout for validated items
Direct brand partnerships = credibility + selective assortment
NETSEA / goooods / SUPER DELIVERY / orosy = no/low-fixed-cost product discovery and low-lot sourcing
Printful = zero-inventory RTB original test
Shopify Collective = later-stage option only after repeat sales justify Shopify's recurring plan
OEM = only after repeat demand is proven

Core operating rule:
ASK → TEST → PRESELL → BUY → FULFIL → MEASURE → SCALE

## 1. Market reality

Japan sports-goods market:
- 2025 domestic shipment estimate: ¥1.73131 trillion, +3.1% YoY.
- Growth is not pure demand growth; price increases are an important contributor.

Japan sports-apparel market:
- 2025 estimate: ¥644.23 billion, +3.1% YoY.
- Outdoor apparel, inbound demand, and price increases are key drivers.

Implication for RTB:
Do not assume “sports is growing, therefore product will sell.”
RTB must validate each category with paid demand.

Primary public sources:
- https://www.yano.co.jp/press-release/show/press_id/4078
- https://www.yano.co.jp/press-release/show/press_id/4025

## 2. Sendai competitive reality

RTB is surrounded by established sporting-goods retail:
- JN Sports in Wakabayashi handles many sport categories including basketball, running and training wear.
- BALLER'S SENDAI is a basketball-specialty retailer.
- Sports Depo Sendai Shinko is a broad generalist carrying basketball, running, fitness, apparel and support gear.
- Xebio and Step Sports add large-scale multi-sport / running competition.

Therefore RTB must not compete on:
- total assortment
- footwear wall
- general team sporting goods
- price discounting
- “we carry famous brands”

RTB can compete on:
- context: products are demonstrated inside actual training
- recommendation: why a product is selected and who needs it
- low-friction local pickup
- preorder and sample-first retail
- connection to RTB coaching and RBA events
- customer feedback loop before inventory

## 5. Checkout / ecommerce stack

### Phase 1: Square Online Free
Public current pricing:
- ¥0 monthly
- online payment fee 3.6%
- store pickup supported
- online cart / inventory / customer accounts available

Decision:
Use Square first because RTB already uses Square operationally and the pilot cannot justify another recurring ecommerce subscription.

Workflow:
RTB landing page → Square product/preorder page → customer pays → RTB places supplier order → pickup/shipping.

Source:
- https://squareup.com/jp/ja/online-store/plans

### Phase 2: Shopify Collective — only after product proof
Japan and JPY are supported.
Supplier can fulfil directly to RTB's customer.
Shopify states retailer margins commonly range 20–50%.
Collective itself can be free for eligible stores, but an active Shopify plan is required.
Current Basic reference price is ¥3,650/month when billed annually.

Decision:
Do not migrate just for RTB SELECT during the zero-cash pilot.
Reconsider when:
- monthly ecommerce orders are consistent
- supplier partners actually use Shopify Collective
- operational time saved is worth the recurring platform cost

Sources:
- https://www.shopify.com/jp/collective
- https://help.shopify.com/ja/manual/online-sales-channels/shopify-collective/retailers
- https://www.shopify.com/jp/pricing

## 6. RTB original / OEM path

### Stage 0 — POD validation
Printful:
- free registration
- no monthly fee
- no minimum order
- produce only after purchase
- direct fulfillment available

Use:
- RTB STANDARD TEE
- RTB minimal cap / apparel tests
- event-specific designs

Purpose:
Test message, color, size and price.
POD is not the final margin engine.

Source:
- https://www.printful.com/jp/print-on-demand

### Stage 1 — socks OEM only after presale
BLANKET LABO public example:
- MOQ 50 pairs
- 50 pairs: sample ¥20,000 + ¥1,400/pair = ¥90,000 ex-tax/shipping
- 100 pairs: public reference ¥980/pair, plus sample if used
- S/M/L same price
- optional grip / anti-odor / moisture-management
- sample approx 2–3 weeks, mass production approx 4–8 weeks

Implication:
50-pair OEM is not attractive if cash is tight.
At 50 pairs, sample cost materially raises unit cost.
Do not place 50-pair order simply to “launch a brand.”
Trigger OEM only after enough presales to fund it.

Source:
- https://oem.blanketlabo.com/products/socks

### Stage 2 — true apparel OEM
Full custom apparel companies often begin around 50 units.
This is a later move after cut / sizing / fabric demand is validated through third-party or POD sales.

Example source:
- https://airera.net/oemodm/

## 7. Category capital-efficiency ranking

### Priority 1 — repeatable / low inventory complexity
- sports socks
- tape / body-care consumables
- support sleeves / small recovery goods
- underwear
- cap / small accessory

Reasons:
- fewer size/color combinations than full apparel
- easier shipping
- lower ticket risk
- repeat-purchase potential
- compatible with sample/preorder

### Priority 2 — apparel by preorder
- training tee
- shorts
- lightweight long sleeve
- lifestyle tee

Rules:
- preorder first
- max 1–2 colors per test
- do not buy full XS–XXL size runs speculatively

### Priority 3 — display/order only
- Hyperice/Therabody devices
- high-ticket recovery
- OOFOS / footwear
- bags
- expensive outerwear

### Avoid in pilot
- basketball shoes
- broad sneaker assortment
- fashion seasonal collections
- hoodies / jackets with many sizes unless fully presold
- supplements / food products
- anything dependent on aggressive health claims

## 8. Price architecture

The current Japanese basketball-apparel benchmark supports roughly:
- T-shirts around ¥4,180–¥5,500 in major basketball brands.

ballaholic SUMMERBALL 2026 Tee ¥5,500.

Decision:
RTB original tee should not be introduced merely to be cheaper.
Test customer willingness around ¥4,950–¥5,500 only after product quality and identity justify it.

## 9. Initial 3-product pilot logic

Do not choose exact SKUs until demand survey reaches a meaningful sample.

Recommended category shortlist:
A. performance socks
B. training/lifestyle underwear or tee
C. consumable recovery/support item

Why not three apparel garments:
One tee + one shorts + one hoodie creates too much size/color inventory complexity.

## 10. 30-day target

Day 1–7
- 30 qualified demand responses
- contact ALCO / United Sports Brands / SAXX
- create NETSEA / goooods / SUPER DELIVERY / orosy buyer accounts only if terms are acceptable
- no purchase

Day 8–14
- rank requested brands/categories
- obtain actual landed cost for top 5 candidate products
- reject anything requiring excessive opening inventory

Day 15–24
- DROP 01: maximum 3 items
- 5–10 day paid preorder
- use Square Online Free
- no paid advertising

Day 25–30
- order only paid units
- calculate actual gross profit after payment fee, inbound freight and shipping
- collect fit / satisfaction data
- decide TEST / SCALE / STOP

## 11. Metrics that matter

Views and likes are not demand.

Primary:
- completed qualified demand forms
- preorder conversion
- paid orders
- actual revenue
- landed gross profit yen
- gross margin after fees
- unsold inventory yen
- repeat intent
- return / size exchange rate

Secondary:
- page visit → form conversion
- form → paid preorder conversion
- pickup vs shipping split
- category / price / size distributions

## 12. Hard stop rules

Do not stock if:
- supplier requires a large opening order
- demand is mainly compliments, not paid orders
- estimated 30-day sell-through <60%
- landed margin is unattractive
- sizes/colors create excess complexity
- the same item is heavily price-competed locally/online
- the item has no clear RTB recommendation story

Do not OEM if:
- fewer than two successful sales windows
- fewer than 30 units can be presold/committed
- landed gross margin target cannot exceed ~60%
- the product is trend dependent

## 13. Final positioning

RTB SELECT is not:
“A small sports shop inside a gym.”

It is:
“A training environment where selected products are tested, explained and bought only when useful.”

RTB = trust / physical experience
RBA = basketball / events / national reach
SELECT = curated third-party products
ORIGINAL = only what demand proves should exist
