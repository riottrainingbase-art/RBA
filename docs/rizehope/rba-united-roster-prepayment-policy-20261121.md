# RBA UNITED U12 / RIZEHOPE JAPAN 2027 — roster prepayment and fee policy

Decision recorded 2026-11-21: **No separate registration/admin fee; only an event participation fee. Only full advance payment qualifies a player for the confirmed roster.**

## Two intentionally different registrations

1. **Free guardian expression of interest (EOI)** — existing form https://form.jotform.com/263221266794057 ; cost ¥0; not an approved team slot; cannot initiate a charge. No roster slot is reserved at this stage.
2. **Formal individual participation** — after event eligibility, safeguarding/staffing, signed guardian terms, official fee and refund rules are approved, a private, individual payment invitation may be issued to the approved guardian only. **Full payment (100% of the approved participation fee) must be reconciled before the player becomes a member of the final/confirmed roster.**

## No extra registration fee

- Separate registration fee: **¥0**.
- Administrative fee: **¥0** additional to official participation fee.
- If actual platform costs exist, budget them transparently in the published participation fee rather than charging an undisclosed extra line.
- Domestic 2-day team fee ¥66,000 is for a team, **not** for one UNITED player.
- Preliminary UNITED concepts of ¥22,000 early / ¥24,800 regular and ¥10,000 deposit are **NOT approved public prices**, not billable, not proof of an early deadline, and can change only following approval and written notification.

## Confirmation gates (all conditions required)

`free_interest` → `under_review` → `eligible_and_selected_not_confirmed` → `written_terms_accepted` → `awaiting_full_payment` → `full_payment_verified` → **`confirmed_roster`**

**Do not create/submit an officially confirmed roster entry until the full payment is verified and these gates also pass:** official event/team slot, U12 eligibility, guardian written consent, named participating legal contracting entity, tax and cancellation/refund rules, staff/safeguarding/insurance, unique parent-entry-to-payment-ID reconciliation, no duplicate payments. An internal selection list is NOT the confirmed roster.

- Any deposit/part-payment scheme, if offered, merely reserves a provisional application under written terms (subject to the organizer's allocation and refund policy). A deposit alone **never** grants confirmed-roster status. Balance must be paid before the roster deadline.
- Full advance payment is collected only after all participation terms are disclosed to, and agreed by, the responsible guardian; do not charge non-selected families.
- The publication of an invitation, expression of interest, or a Stripe session alone is not a completed payment.
- Payment should have explicit webhook/payment-ledger evidence and a unique guardian/application association.
- On non-acceptance, cancellation or refunds, follow written terms and legal obligations. Never claim blanket “non-refundable registration fees” while terms remain unapproved.

## Status now

Draft event websites/PR may show this policy, but public payment links/roster export may not be turned on until operational gates are verified. The event landing URL is not production-live until tested Preview and production readback confirm it.
