## Release gates for each new region
1. Written venue booking/use category, staffing and costs.
2. Named local organiser and escalation/safeguarding officer, responsible adult chaperones.
3. Legally coherent contract, refunds, cancellation, image permission, emergency response and insurance.
4. Written game regulations, U12 age/birth-year criteria, fatigue/rest plan, referees, scorers and healthcare.
5. Separate form + event ID + payment links and accounting ledger, **never reused from Miyagi**.
6. Confirmed budget / enforceable sponsorship funding and adequate cash buffer.
7. International team count only announced once formally accepted, no FIBA/JBA endorsement without evidence.
8. Preview/QA/production release under repository DEPLOYMENT.md; do not deploy from this research update.
