## Sponsorship contract schedule (minimum)
1. Legal identities, signatories, country, tax residency and invoice issuer.
2. Programme, city, tentative/confirmed dates, sponsor amount and currency, tax treatment, payment due date and payment account.
3. Deliverables: exact logos/placements, usage period, channels, approved copy, report date, whether in-kind and valuation.
4. Costs and restricted use: participant support, venue, coaching, interpreter, safety; no diversion to unrelated brands or JPNentry/RTB without express agreement.
5. Conditions precedent: host contract, venue, child-safeguarding, insurance, lawful travel and paid work route.
6. Cancellation: full/partial cancellation, alternatives, nonrecoverable documented costs, refund timing and decision authority.
7. Privacy, media consent, sponsor content review, prohibition on sponsor direct marketing to minors.
8. Term, no automatic renewal, no exclusivity unless specifically agreed, governing law/dispute resolution reviewed locally.
9. Financial and delivery reporting; receipts and reconciled provider settlements; corrective action for under-delivery.

## Financial controls
- Gross programme fees (participant) separate from corporate sponsor fees and in-kind value.
- For in-kind: track fair-value evidence and matched expense savings; do not report cash received.
- Direct costs must be based on quotes/invoices, not old planning figures.
- Sponsor deposits/paid amounts distinguished from bank settled; refundable amounts reserved.
- Sponsor receipts are not automatically 'profit'. Contribution = programme fees + earned sponsor revenue - cash direct costs - contracted sponsor deliverable costs; exclude noncash support from cash forecast.
- Report by country/operator and programme; do not double-book revenue across RBA and SPARTANS.
- No claiming Taiwanese donation tax benefits or official government sports donation platform access without eligible local applicant and legal confirmation.
