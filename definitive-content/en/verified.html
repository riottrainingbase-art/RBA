Trust,
Turn it into a standard, not a feeling.

RBA Verified does not claim that everything carrying the RBA name is automatically safe. It is a quality framework for increasing the information participants can verify and the standards organisers are expected to follow.

DEVELOPMENT

Development appropriate to the age group.

FEEDBACK

Feedback and improvement after participation.

RBA Verified is being developed in stages. The label itself does not guarantee safety or outcomes. Please review each event’s terms, organiser, insurance and cancellation policy before joining.
