STEP 01Notice

U10: open space. U12: teammates and defenders. U15: defender orientation and help position.

STEP 02Choose

During regular practice, consider whether to pass, drive or shoot. Do not judge the choice only by the outcome.

STEP 03Reflect

What did I notice? What did I choose? What will I try next? Guardians can listen without supplying the answer.
