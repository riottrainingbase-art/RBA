RBA POLICIES

Trust,
by design.

RBA Verified is not a badge. It is an operating standard designed to help players, teams and communities make informed decisions.

POLICY FRAMEWORK

Trust is built through operations, not slogans.

CHILD SAFETY

For every programme, we review child safety, appropriate communication, photo/video handling and emergency procedures.

PRICE TRANSPARENCY

We aim to make fees, additional costs and cancellation terms clear before registration.

DATA MINIMIZATION

We limit personal data collection—especially information about minors, health and emergency contacts—to what is necessary.

TRAVEL SCOPE

We keep basketball programming and travel arrangements clearly separated, with responsibilities assigned to appropriate providers for each project.

CLAIMS

We clearly separate current results, relationships under discussion and future vision. Unconfirmed information is never presented as an achievement.

INCIDENTS

Our priority is not to hide incidents, but to ensure reporting, response and prevention systems work.

This page sets out RBA’s public operating principles. Formal participation terms, cancellation rules and consent requirements for individual events take precedence on each event’s registration page.

PAYMENT PRINCIPLE

When do I pay?

Application, confirmation and payment are separate steps. RBA-owned eligible programmes use Stripe after server-side registration validation. Accommodation/transport-sensitive programmes, Team Training, International Exchange and future external Organizer transactions are not treated as generic public instant checkout.
