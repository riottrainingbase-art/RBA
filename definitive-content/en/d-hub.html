RBA D-HUB / DEVELOPMENT PROGRAMMES

Develop. Connect. Empower.

Learn, apply, observe and reflect. D-HUB offers two ongoing development programmes: COACH LAB for coaches and PLAYERS for young athletes. You can add a development environment without changing teams.

COACH LAB (Japanese details)PLAYERS日本語

Each programme is ¥3,300 per month (tax included), with monthly automatic renewal. Applying does not itself trigger payment. Already a member? Use your existing RBA ID; do not pay again for the same programme.

WEEKLY FORMAT

WEEKLY ONLINE COACHING LESSON

A weekly learning format of 30–45 minutes. One coaching question at a time. Learn it, test it on court, observe what changes, and bring the result back the following week.
