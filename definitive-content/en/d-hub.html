Try the learning cycle before joining.

These original RBA practice examples are free to read, without registration or payment. Add one observation and reflection to your normal practice, not extra training volume.

COACH LABObserve what players see before receiving

In a supervised 2-on-2 game, vary the starting position of the defender. Observe whether the player notices the defender and available space before receiving. Ask why they chose to pass, drive or shoot; do not judge the decision solely by the result.

Practice: 5 minutes observation, 10 minutes play, 5 minutes discussion, 5 minutes modified play, 5 minutes reflection. Adjust to age, ability, space and fatigue.

PLAYERSWhat can I see before the catch?

During regular training, look for teammates, defenders and available space. After one practice, explain what you noticed, what you chose, and what you might try next.
