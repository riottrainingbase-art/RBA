ONE PLATFORM / THREE SUPPLY LAYERS

RBA does not do everything itself.

01 / RBA OWNED

Clinics, development camps, cups, team training and exchange programmes directly designed and quality-managed by RBA.

02 / RBA HOSTED

Co-host locally with Regional Hosts, sharing RBA’s development, safety and operating standards so the model can be repeated across Japan.

03 / MARKETPLACE

Over time, trusted organisers outside RBA can participate too, so players can compare and discover development opportunities across providers.

MEASURABLE DEVELOPMENT

STANDARDS BEFORE SCALE

Standards first.
Scale second.

Trust should come from repeatable standards and visible accountability — not from size alone. Development, safeguarding, operations, data and international exchange are being built as systems, not personal know-how.
