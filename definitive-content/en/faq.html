FAQ

Do not ask people
to register without clarity.

Before anyone joins, we want them to understand what RBA does—and what it does not do.

FIELDNETWORKASIA
Is RBA a basketball school?

Not only. We are building a development platform connecting clinics, camps, cups, team training, domestic and international exchange, and MY HOME COURT.

Do players need to change teams?

No. RBA is designed to add the next development opportunity to a player’s current environment, not take away their existing team.

Is MY HOME COURT a paid video service?

No. It is a free-to-register Basketball Passport for finding programmes, registering, paying and keeping participation history. Terms are confirmed separately each time you join a programme.

Does RBA sell overseas trips as travel packages?

RBA focuses on basketball programming and exchange design. Accommodation and transport are generally handled through appropriate travel providers or direct contracts with service providers, depending on the project.

Can external organisers use RBA?

Our long-term goal is a marketplace where trusted external events meeting RBA standards can also be discovered. We are building that system in stages.

Does “one of Japan’s leading platforms” describe RBA today?

No. It describes our vision, not a current claim. Current results and future targets are clearly separated on the site.

When do I pay?

You apply first. Eligible day clinics and online programmes can continue to secure Stripe Checkout after an RBA registration is created. Programmes requiring accommodation, transport, availability review or individual scope are confirmed before payment.
