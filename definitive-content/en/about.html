RBA — Youth Basketball Development Platform
PURPOSE

Growing opportunities, from local courts to the world.

RBA is a basketball development organisation creating opportunities for young people to grow and take on new challenges. We connect player coaching, games, physical preparation, coach education and international exchange, opening learning opportunities beyond a player’s home team or region.

MISSION

A next opportunity to grow, for every young player.

RBA is a basketball development organisation creating opportunities for young people to grow and take on new challenges. We connect player coaching, games, physical preparation, coach education and international exchange, opening learning opportunities beyond a player’s home team or region.

VISION

Building one of Japan’s leading youth basketball development platforms.

WHAT RBA DOES

Five connected areas of development.

One experience can support everyday growth. We connect courts in Japan and Asia so players and coaches can keep learning together.

Player development

Connect technique, decisions and movement to the game through clinics, camps and ongoing coaching.

Explore →

Games and development environments

Through BACKBONE and 3x3, create opportunities for every participant to play, try and reflect.

Explore →

Coach education

Share observation, practice design and ways of supporting young players through workshops, D-HUB and resources.

Explore →

Physical preparation

Work with RTB on age-appropriate strength and conditioning, injury prevention and return to sport.

International exchange

Build relationships through learning with players and coaches in other countries, including our work in Taiwan and Malaysia.
